Yunix Community Guidelines

Effective date: 21 January 2026

Last updated: 21 January 2026

Yunix (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is a location-based social app. To keep Yunix safe, we have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behaviour.

These Community Guidelines apply to all user-generated content and interactions on Yunix, including messages, images, profiles, and any other content you create.

1. What’s not allowed

You must not create, upload, send, or promote content that includes:

Harassment & abuse

  • Bullying, harassment, stalking, intimidation, or unwanted contact
  • Threats of violence or harm
  • Hate speech or discrimination (based on race, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or similar protected characteristics)
  • Doxxing or sharing someone’s private information without permission

Sexual content & exploitation

  • Any sexual content involving minors (this will be removed and may be reported to relevant authorities)
  • Sexual exploitation, coercion, non-consensual sexual content, or “revenge porn”
  • Requests for sexual content or sexual services in exchange for money, gifts, or anything of value (solicitation)

Violence & self-harm

  • Threats, encouragement, or instructions to harm yourself or others
  • Graphic violence or gore

Illegal or dangerous activity

  • Content that promotes illegal activity
  • Instructions or encouragement for dangerous behaviour that could lead to harm

Spam, scams, and deception

  • Spam, mass messaging, or repeated unwanted requests
  • Fraud, phishing, misleading links, or attempts to collect personal data
  • Impersonation or pretending to be someone else in order to deceive or abuse users

Privacy violations

  • Sharing someone else’s personal data (phone number, address, identity documents) without consent
  • Sharing private images of someone without their permission

2. Respect consent and boundaries

  • If someone asks you to stop contacting them, you must stop.
  • If someone blocks you, do not attempt to evade the block or contact them through alternative accounts.
  • Do not pressure or coerce users into sharing personal information, images, or location details.

3. Reporting and blocking

Yunix includes safety tools such as blocking and reporting.

  • You can report a message or image from within the app.
  • You can block a user to prevent further interaction.

Reports may include the reason you select, any description you provide, and the relevant content needed to investigate.

4. Enforcement

If you violate these guidelines, Yunix may take action, including:

  • Removing content
  • Issuing a warning
  • Temporarily restricting features
  • Suspending the account
  • Permanently banning the account
  • Preserving evidence where needed for safety or legal reasons

Severe violations (for example, threats, exploitation, or content involving minors) may result in immediate action without warning.

5. Emergency and safety note

If you are in immediate danger, call 999. Yunix is not an emergency service.
