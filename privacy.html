11. Safety, moderation and safeguarding

Yunix includes safety tools such as blocking and reporting.

Blocking another user will:

  • Remove each other as friends
  • Remove each other from messages
  • Disable location transfer between the two users
  • Disable any form of communication and location broadcasting between the users

Reports are reviewed and may result in content removal, account restrictions, or bans.

If you are in immediate danger, call 999. Yunix is not an emergency service.
